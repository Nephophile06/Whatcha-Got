# Design & UX Decisions

Throughout the development of the "Whatcha Got?" prototype, several key UX decisions were made to ensure the product remains user-centered and practical.

## 1. Reducing Decision Effort
* **Context:** The initial problem of “what should I eat?” could easily become a very large product with too many features.
* **Decision:** Focused the experience strictly around four key inputs: ingredients, time, mood, and budget. This constraint prevents choice paralysis.

## 2. Clear Call-to-Actions (CTAs)
* **Context:** During V1, users hesitated because buttons had vague, playful texts (e.g., "Let's do this!").
* **Decision:** Replaced playful microcopy with clear, action-oriented labels (e.g., "Save Goal", "Share Recipe"). Clarity was prioritized over cleverness.

## 3. Persistent Input Labels
* **Context:** Early designs relied on disappearing placeholder texts for input fields, which failed the accessibility contrast audit (3.9:1) and caused memory strain.
* **Decision:** Increased contrast to 7.5:1 and ensured labels remain visible even when the user is typing.
