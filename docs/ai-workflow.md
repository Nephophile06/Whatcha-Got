# AI-Assisted Workflow

AI was used as a supporting tool throughout the design process—not as a replacement for design decisions or user validation.

## Top Time-Saving Prompts
1. **UX Ideation:** "Help me explore practical UX solutions for users who have ingredients at home but don't know what to cook." (Saved initial brainstorming time).
2. **Edge Case Exploration:** "Review this meal-planning flow and identify possible empty, error, loading, offline, and validation states." (Helped build a robust UX).
3. **Documentation:** "Review this user flow from a usability perspective and identify areas where the user may hesitate." (Aided in planning usability testing).

## Failed Prompt & Lesson Learned
* **Prompt:** "Design the best meal-planning experience for my app."
* **Result:** The output was too generic and disconnected from the specific target users and constraints.
* **Lesson:** Specific context produces more useful AI output. AI requires the target user, product context, and constraints to function effectively as a thinking partner.
